\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{Contract value metrics}}\\
$G_t^m$ &
Hourly aggregate metered import: $\sum_i I_{i,t}^m$ in $\mathcal R,\mathcal I$;
$P_t^{\mathrm{imp},\mathcal C}$ in $\mathcal C$ (including exceedance
in both contract models) &
kWh\\
$\Delta J^{m\mid\mathcal R}$ &
Community cost saving relative to no contract,
$J^{\mathcal R}-J^m$, for $m\in\{\mathcal I,\mathcal C\}$ &
DKK\\
$\Delta J^{\mathcal C\mid\mathcal I}$ &
Additional saving from collective rather than individual contracts,
$J^{\mathcal I}-J^{\mathcal C}$ &
DKK\\
$V^m$ &
Daily aggregate import exceedance,
$\sum_t[G_t^m-\overline P_t^{\mathrm{DSO}}]_+$ &
kWh\\
$P_{\mathrm{peak}}^m$ &
Maximum hourly import, $\max_t G_t^m$,
expressed as average power under the one-hour convention &
kW\\
$H^m$ &
Number of one-hour intervals whose import exceeds the cap by more
than $\varepsilon$ &
h\\
$\varepsilon$ &
Numerical tolerance for identifying hours above the cap:
$2\times10^{-6}$ &
kWh\\
$\mathbf{1}\{\cdot\}$ &
Indicator equal to one if the stated condition holds and zero otherwise &
--\\
