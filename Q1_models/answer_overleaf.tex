\subsection{Mathematical formulations and problem classes}
Members are indexed by $i\in\mathcal N$, and $t\in\mathcal T=\{0,\ldots,23\}$.
The interval is one hour; conversions of battery power and contractual
capacity into hourly energy are implicit. Define
\begin{align}
 \ell^{\rm ref}_{i,t}&=L^{\max}\rho_t,&
 \underline\ell_{i,t}&=\max\{0,\ell^{\rm ref}_{i,t}-d^{\max}\},&
 \overline\ell_{i,t}&=\min\{L^{\max},\ell^{\rm ref}_{i,t}+d^{\max}\},\\
 E_i^{\min}&=\sum_t\ell^{\rm ref}_{i,t},&
 D_i&=\sum_t c_i^L(d^+_{i,t}+d^-_{i,t}).
\end{align}
Here $L^{\max}=3$ kWh/h, $d^{\max}=1$ kWh and $c_i^L>0$.
Let $\mathcal X_i$ consist of
\begin{align}
 \underline\ell_{i,t}\le\ell_{i,t}&\le\overline\ell_{i,t},&
 \sum_t\ell_{i,t}&\ge E_i^{\min},\\
 \ell_{i,t}-\ell^{\rm ref}_{i,t}&=d^+_{i,t}-d^-_{i,t},&
 d^+_{i,t},d^-_{i,t}&\ge0,\\
 0\le P_{i,t}&\le\overline P_{i,t},&
 0\le p^{\rm ch}_{i,t}&\le\overline P_i^{\rm ch},\\
 0\le p^{\rm dis}_{i,t}&\le\overline P_i^{\rm dis},&
 0\le e_{i,t}&\le\overline E_i,\\
 e_{i,0}&=E_i^0+\rho_i^{\rm ch}p^{\rm ch}_{i,0}
                         -p^{\rm dis}_{i,0}/\rho_i^{\rm dis},\\
 e_{i,t}&=e_{i,t-1}+\rho_i^{\rm ch}p^{\rm ch}_{i,t}
                         -p^{\rm dis}_{i,t}/\rho_i^{\rm dis},\quad t=1,\ldots,23,\\
 e_{i,23}&\ge E_i^0,\\
 q_{i,t}&=\ell_{i,t}-P_{i,t}+p^{\rm ch}_{i,t}-p^{\rm dis}_{i,t},
 \qquad q_{i,t}\in\mathbb R.
\end{align}
All hourly constraints apply for $t\in\mathcal T$, unless stated otherwise.
Members without an asset have its availability/capacity/power limits set to zero.
In $\mathcal R$ and $\mathcal I$, $q=I-E$ denotes individually metered net
import. In $\mathcal C$, $q$ is internal net demand, and only the community
meter incurs external trading costs and grid tariffs.

The full and discounted trading prices are
\begin{align}
 a_t&=p_t+\tau^{\rm imp},& b_t&=p_t-\tau^{\rm exp},\\
 a_t^{\rm d}&=p_t+(1-\beta^{\rm DSO})\tau^{\rm imp},&
 b_t^{\rm d}&=p_t-(1-\beta^{\rm DSO})\tau^{\rm exp}.
\end{align}
Discounted tariffs apply to all metered imports and exports. An additional
penalty $\alpha^{\rm DSO}>0$ DKK/kWh applies to imports above the cap.

\paragraph{Reference model $\mathcal R$.}
\begin{align}
 J^{\mathcal R}=\min\quad&\sum_{i,t}
 [c_iP_{i,t}+a_tI_{i,t}-b_tE_{i,t}+c_i^L(d^+_{i,t}+d^-_{i,t})],\\
 \mathrm{s.t.}\quad&\mathcal X_i,\quad i\in\mathcal N,\\
 &q_{i,t}-I_{i,t}+E_{i,t}=0,\quad I_{i,t},E_{i,t}\ge0.
\end{align}
The cap is only a reference for ex-post congestion measurement in this model.
Separate metering is preserved; the summed optimization is separable.

\paragraph{Individual contracts $\mathcal I$.}
First solve $\mathcal R$ and fix
\begin{equation}
 n_i^{\mathcal R}=\left[\sum_t(I^{\mathcal R}_{i,t}-E^{\mathcal R}_{i,t})\right]_+,
 \quad \kappa_i=\frac{n_i^{\mathcal R}}{\sum_j n_j^{\mathcal R}},
 \quad \overline P^{\rm DSO}_{i,t}=\kappa_i\overline P_t^{\rm DSO}.
\end{equation}
The denominator must be positive. Then
\begin{align}
 J^{\mathcal I}=\min\quad&\sum_{i,t}[c_iP_{i,t}+a_t^{\rm d}I_{i,t}
 -b_t^{\rm d}E_{i,t}+c_i^L(d^+_{i,t}+d^-_{i,t})+\alpha^{\rm DSO}x_{i,t}],\\
 \mathrm{s.t.}\quad&\mathcal X_i,\quad i\in\mathcal N,\\
 &q_{i,t}-I_{i,t}+E_{i,t}=0,\\
 &I_{i,t}-x_{i,t}\le\kappa_i\overline P_t^{\rm DSO},
 \quad I_{i,t},E_{i,t},x_{i,t}\ge0.
\end{align}

\paragraph{Centralized community $\mathcal C$.}
\begin{align}
 J^{\mathcal C}=\min\quad&\sum_{i,t}[c_iP_{i,t}+c_i^L(d^+_{i,t}+d^-_{i,t})]
 +\sum_t[a_t^{\rm d}P_t^{\rm imp}-b_t^{\rm d}P_t^{\rm exp}+\alpha^{\rm DSO}x_t],\\
 \mathrm{s.t.}\quad&\mathcal X_i,\quad i\in\mathcal N,\\
 &\sum_i q_{i,t}-P_t^{\rm imp}+P_t^{\rm exp}=0,\\
 &P_t^{\rm imp}-x_t\le\overline P_t^{\rm DSO},
 \quad P_t^{\rm imp},P_t^{\rm exp},x_t\ge0.
\end{align}
The models minimize procurement cost plus disutility; for $\mathcal C$ this
is equivalent to maximizing welfare $W^{\mathcal C}=-J^{\mathcal C}$ under
the reference utility normalization. Each is an LP. Base-case feasibility
and boundedness imply strong duality and necessary and sufficient KKT conditions.
